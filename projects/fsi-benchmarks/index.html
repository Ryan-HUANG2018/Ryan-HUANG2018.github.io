FSI benchmarks — flexible filament and flapping flag

A flexible filament in a uniform flow (2D) and a flapping flag (3D), solved with my coupled immersed boundary–lattice Boltzmann and structural solvers, and compared with published results.

REYNOLDS NUMBER (2D)
100
MASS RATIO (2D)
1
STRETCHING / BENDING (2D)
500 / 0.0001
METHOD
IB–LBM + own structural solvers

Setup

Slender elastic bodies coupled to the flow are a standard test of a fluid–structure interaction solver. Both cases use my immersed boundary–lattice Boltzmann (IB–LBM) flow solver, coupled to my own structural solvers (see Structural solver verification).

  • 2D flexible filament in a uniform flow at Re = 100, with mass ratio 1, stretching stiffness 500 and bending stiffness 0.0001.
  • 3D flapping flag: a flexible plate in a uniform flow, with the same IB–LBM coupling.

Both cases are described in detail in my PhD thesis (UNSW, 2021).

Results

Flexible filament. The transverse (y) and streamwise (x) positions of the trailing end follow the results of Tian et al. and Wang et al. (2017).

Flapping flag. The flag motion matches Tian et al. (2014).

Publication

Huang, Q. (2021). Low Reynolds Number Turbulent FSI and its Applications in Biological Flows. PhD thesis, UNSW Canberra, University of New South Wales. doi:10.26190/unsworks/3993
