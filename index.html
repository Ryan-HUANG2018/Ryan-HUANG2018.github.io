SOLVER RUNNING · QUT · Centre for Biomedical Technologies

Qiuxiang (Ryan) Huang

Computational fluid dynamics — fluid–structure interaction and AI-driven modelling for biomedical, aerospace and energy applications.

Visiting Fellow in the Cardiovascular Technology Laboratory, Centre for Biomedical Technologies, Queensland University of Technology (QUT). I develop high-fidelity fluid–structure interaction solvers and use them to study cardiovascular flows, insect flight and flapping-wing micro air vehicles.
